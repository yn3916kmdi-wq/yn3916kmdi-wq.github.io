Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 30, 2026

This policy explains how Nomasa Yusuke ("we") handles information in the mobile app "e-MyCloset" (the "App"). If this English version differs from the Japanese version, the Japanese version prevails.

1. Our approach

The App stores your clothing photos, outfits and wear records only on your device. We don't operate any servers and never receive this information.

2. Information stored on your device

  • Images of your clothes (cutouts) and their name, category, colors, seasons, purchase date, size, price, notes, tags, etc.
  • Outfits, wear records, photos attached to the calendar or outfits, and packing lists
  • App settings (language, theme, week start, currency, morning reminder time, etc.)

This information is deleted from your device when you delete the App or choose More → Erase all data. We cannot recover it.

3. Camera and photos

The App uses the camera and your photos so you can photograph or choose clothes. Cutouts are processed on your device, and photos are never uploaded. Images are saved to your photo library only when you choose to.

4. Notifications

The morning reminder is a notification scheduled on your device. No information is sent anywhere for it.

5. Communication with external services

(1) Loading the cutout engine

To improve cutout accuracy, the App may download Google's MediaPipe library and models from jsDelivr (a CDN) and Google's servers. Your photos are not sent, but general connection information such as your IP address is sent to these services as part of the request.

(2) Ads (Google AdMob)

On the free plan, the App shows ads using Google AdMob, provided by Google LLC. For ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention, Google AdMob may collect:

  • Device identifiers such as the advertising ID
  • IP address (which may be used to estimate your approximate location)
  • App usage such as ad impressions and taps
  • Diagnostic information about the App and SDK performance

In regions where consent is required (such as the EEA), we ask for your consent before showing ads, and you can change it in More → Ad privacy settings. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. See Google's policy for how Google uses this information. Premium doesn't show ads or load the ad SDK.

(3) Premium purchases (App Store, Google Play, RevenueCat)

Premium purchases and payments are handled by Apple or Google. We never receive payment details such as credit card numbers. To verify and manage purchases, we use RevenueCat, provided by RevenueCat, Inc., which receives your purchase history and an anonymous user ID created by the App. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

6. Backups

Backup files you create with More → Create backup are saved where you choose (iCloud Drive, Google Drive, email, etc.). We never receive them.

7. Sharing with third parties

Except for the collection by the services described in section 5, we don't share your information with third parties, unless required by law.

8. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13.

9. Deletion requests

You can delete the information on your device with More → Erase all data or by deleting the App. To request deletion of purchase history held by RevenueCat, contact us using the details below.

10. If you live in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland

The following information is provided under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and similar laws.

  • Controller: We are responsible for how information is handled in the App (see section 12 for our contact details). For how information is used for ads, please also see Google's policy.
  • Legal bases: Information is processed for ads on the basis of your consent where the law requires consent, and otherwise on the basis of our legitimate interests in delivering ads and preventing fraud. You can change or withdraw your consent at any time in More → Ad privacy settings. Purchases are verified and managed (RevenueCat) to perform our contract with you to provide Premium.
  • Your rights: You have the right to access, rectify or erase your information, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, to withdraw your consent, and to lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority in your country. We don't hold your information, so you can view and delete the information on your device in the App yourself. For any other request, contact us using the details in section 12.
  • International transfers: Google and RevenueCat may process information outside the EEA, including in the United States. Such transfers rely on the safeguards described in each company's privacy policy.
  • Retention: Information on your device is kept until you delete it. Information collected by Google and RevenueCat is kept according to their policies.

11. Changes to this policy

We will announce any changes to this policy on this page.
