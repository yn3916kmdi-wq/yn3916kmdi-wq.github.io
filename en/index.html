e-MyCloset

Snap, cut out, and style your clothes

e-MyCloset cuts your clothes out of a photo automatically, so you can manage your closet, create outfits and keep a daily log of what you wore. Everything you add is stored only on your device.

FAQ

Where is my data stored?

Everything is stored on your device. Nothing is sent to our servers (we don't have any). Deleting the app also deletes your data.

Can I move my data to a new phone?

On your old phone, go to More → Create backup and save the file to iCloud Drive, Google Drive, email, etc. On your new phone, choose More → Restore from backup and pick that file. This works between iPhone and Android too.

How do I cancel Premium?

Cancel from your store account settings. If you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, you won't be charged for the next one. Deleting the app does not cancel your subscription.

  • iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions
  • Android: Google Play Store → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions
How do I get Premium back after reinstalling or changing phones?

Sign in with the same Apple ID or Google account you used to buy it, then choose More → Restore purchases.

The cutout doesn't look right

Place the item on a plain wall or floor and keep the whole item in the photo. Drawing a frame around the item before starting improves accuracy, and you can touch up the result with the eraser and restore tools.

I don't get the morning reminder

Check that notifications for e-MyCloset are allowed in your device settings. Battery-saving settings may delay notifications.

Can I group my clothes (tags)?

When adding or editing an item, use Tags to create and add tags such as “Work” or “Weekend”. Tap a tag at the top of your closet to show only the items with that tag. To rename or delete tags, go to More → Settings → Manage tags.

Where can I see prices and cost per wear?

Add a price when editing an item to see its cost per wear on the item page. More → Wear insights also shows the total value of your closet. You can change the currency in More → Settings → Currency.

How do I remove ads?

Premium removes ads from every screen.
